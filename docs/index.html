How scoring works

The rule engine scores 0-100. A score of 0-19 is clean, 20-39 is low, 40-69 is suspicious, and 70-100 is malicious. Many weak hits accumulate; a single critical hit can land high on its own. The letter grade on this report is a cosmetic re-slicing of those same bands: A (0-19), B (20-39), C (40-54), D (55-69), F (70-100) -- A is safest, F is most dangerous.

When the optional Claude judge runs, the final score is the higher of the rule score and the judge score. A "dampened" note means the text matched documentation vocabulary (an article about prompt injection, not one performing it) and its rule score was lowered accordingly -- always reported, never silent.
